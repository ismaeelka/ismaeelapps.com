Privacy Policy

Last updated: 15 November 2026

Your game. Your privacy.

Load Parade is developed and operated by Ismaeel (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle information when you play Load Parade, use an enabled online feature, or contact us.

Local gameplay: progress is saved on your device. Advertising depends on the version you use. Newer builds use Google AdMob and Unity Ads for optional rewarded videos. Apple processes purchases on iOS and Google Play processes purchases in Android builds that include Play Billing. These local releases do not use Firebase accounts, cloud-save synchronization, hosted purchase verification, remote configuration, push notifications, or separate game analytics and crash reporting services.

1. Game and profile information

Load Parade stores information needed to run the game, including a randomly generated player/profile identifier, level progress, Coins, Lives, worker upgrades, Ability inventory, time-based benefits, settings, and reward or transaction records. Local records help restore your progress and prevent the same action or reward from being applied twice.

Normal gameplay in this release keeps these records on your device. A player identifier is a support reference; it is not your real name and is not a password. The game does not require your real name or email address to play its local levels.

2. Online services and security

Puzzle play and saved progress work locally without a game account or hosted game service. Purchase and restore requests to Apple or Google Play, and permitted advertising and consent requests, require a connection. Their providers receive the technical information needed for those requests.

Firebase Authentication, App Check, Analytics and Crashlytics, CloudKit recovery, public leaderboards, remote configuration and push services are not included in this local release. Owner/developer diagnostic tests in separate builds are not automatic collection from ordinary players.

3. In-app purchases

On iOS, Apple processes payment through the App Store. We do not receive your payment-card number. The app verifies purchases using StoreKit on the device and keeps local product/transaction identifiers, purchase status, purchase time and delivery records to prevent duplicate grants.

Eligible permanent products can be restored through Apple. Local progress, consumable balances and used rewards do not have a hosted backup or cross-device recovery in this release. Apple Sandbox/TestFlight purchases are tests, not public charges. Refund/revocation reconciliation uses evidence available from Apple on the device; no game purchase-verification server is used.

Apple handles its own account and payment information under its App Store privacy notice.

On Android, Google Play processes payments in Internal testing builds that include Play Billing. Google handles account and payment information under the Google Privacy Policy. The game sends Google Play a coded profile identifier and purchase reference to match purchases with the correct local profile. The game verifies Play receipts on the device and stores product identifiers, hashed purchase tokens, and delivery records to prevent duplicate grants. The game does not receive your payment-card number.

Google Play can restore eligible products it still lists as owned. Consumable balances, used rewards, and local progress stay with the local profile. Google Play handles requests for refunds. Google Play license testers can use the test payment methods shown by Google Play.

4. Advertising and consent

Load Parade uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP). Android Internal testing builds using mediation and the iOS App Store distribution build 1200328 use Unity Ads through AdMob to provide optional rewarded videos. These builds use contextual Unity Ads settings, disable publisher ad personalization and publisher first-party identifiers, and set Google’s maximum ad content rating to G. Earlier iOS releases apply child-restricted, non-personalized Google ad settings and may show Home banners or limited interstitials. On those releases, Remove Ads suppresses banners and interstitials. TestFlight and the first Android internal build use Google sample ads.

These mediated builds refresh Google’s consent information at launch. When Google requires a privacy form, the form must complete and UMP must permit ad requests before the app initializes the ad SDK or requests an ad. The game’s privacy settings provide Google’s privacy options where required. Local gameplay remains available when ads cannot run.

Google and Unity may receive technical information to deliver and measure ads, prevent fraud, and operate their SDKs. This may include IP addresses, approximate location derived from them, app and device identifiers such as Android advertising identifiers where available, interactions with ads, and diagnostic and performance data. Test ads also involve provider processing. Load Parade does not request precise GPS location or ask for Apple tracking permission in these builds.

Video rewards are optional. The game records an earned reward on the device and delivers it once. Skipped or unearned videos grant no reward.

For provider practices, see the Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, Google’s ad SDK disclosures for Android and iOS, and the Unity Privacy Policy.

5. Support information

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, player identifier, app version, device/operating-system information, or screenshots. The in-game support action prepares an email for you to review and send; it does not silently submit a support ticket.

We use this information to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords, recovery secrets, payment-card details, or private authentication keys.

6. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by Apple, Google/AdMob, Unity Ads, and providers needed to deliver an enabled service or respond to support. We may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent fraud, or secure the service.

We do not sell your local game profile for money. Advertising-provider processing can fall within broader legal definitions of data sharing, depending on the feature, configuration, and jurisdiction; we do not treat this statement as an exemption from applicable privacy choices.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their applicable agreements and privacy notices describe their international-transfer safeguards. See the privacy notices from Apple, Google, and Unity.

7. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay records normally remain while you retain the profile on your device. Removing the app may remove local saves, but it does not automatically erase provider records, App Store or Google Play purchase history, protected credentials, or any separately held support information.

We retain support and enabled online-service records only as needed for their stated purposes and applicable legal, security, or transaction obligations. A minimum transaction or abuse-prevention record may need to remain after other profile information is deleted, for example to prevent duplicate purchase delivery or process a refund. Providers may have separate retention periods.

You can request information or deletion using the contact below. We may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request relates to your profile, but will not ask you to email your password or recovery secret. A request is not a confirmation that deletion has completed. There is no hosted game account in this release. Local profile deletion is available in the game; it does not delete App Store or Google Play purchase history, Google or Unity provider records, or separately held support messages.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the services you use, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

Where applicable, information needed to provide a requested feature is processed to provide that service; security and fraud-prevention processing relies on legitimate interests; optional consent-based processing relies on your consent; and legally required records rely on applicable obligations. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Use the game’s Privacy page to reopen Google privacy options where required. You can contact us about privacy requests. This release does not request Apple tracking permission.

9. Children and younger players

Load Parade is a colorful puzzle game. The game does not ask for a birth date or infer that a player is an adult. Local gameplay does not require a real name or email. The advertising settings for each release are described above. Ad providers may process the technical data described there to deliver permitted ads.

Younger players should not send personal information through support without a parent or guardian's involvement. A parent or guardian who believes a child supplied personal information can contact us to request review and deletion where applicable.

10. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the features being provided, including access controls and protected authentication mechanisms for enabled online services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device protected and do not share private recovery or authentication information.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy when game features, service providers, or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest version. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before materially different processing begins.
