Privacy Policy

Last updated: 3 October 2026

Your game. Your privacy.

Load Parade is developed and operated by Ismaeel (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle information when you play Load Parade, use an enabled online feature, or contact us.

Current development version: normal gameplay uses local saves. Advertising is limited to Google test ads in isolated adult-testing sessions. Live advertising, normal online account recovery, public leaderboards, cloud-save synchronization, and hosted purchase verification are not currently enabled in normal gameplay. This policy will be updated when those practices change.

1. Game and profile information

Load Parade stores information needed to run the game, including a randomly generated player/profile identifier, level progress, Coins, Lives, worker upgrades, Ability inventory, time-based benefits, settings, and reward or transaction records. Local records help restore your progress and prevent the same action or reward from being applied twice.

Normal gameplay in the current development version keeps these records on your device. A player identifier is a support reference; it is not your real name and is not a password. The game does not require your real name or email address to play its local levels.

2. Online services and security

When an online feature is enabled and you use it, network requests may include your IP address, app/version information, profile references, authentication information, and the information needed for that feature. These are used to authenticate requests, prevent abuse, and provide the requested service.

Firebase Authentication and App Check

Our isolated online tests use Google Firebase Authentication and App Check. Authentication provides an anonymous service identifier and authentication tokens. App Check uses app/device attestation to help verify that a request comes from an authentic app. Google and the attestation provider may process associated network and technical information. These services are not a public leaderboard or an advertising profile.

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics are not enabled in the current configuration. Device performance evidence collected during owner/developer testing is separate from automatic collection from ordinary players.

Read Firebase’s privacy and security information for details about its processing and service-specific retention.

3. In-app purchases

Where in-app purchases are available, Apple processes payment through the App Store. We do not receive your payment-card number. Purchase-related records may include product and transaction identifiers, purchase status, purchase time, verification evidence, and a reference linking delivery to the appropriate game profile.

These records are used to deliver purchased benefits, prevent duplicate grants, restore eligible purchases, and handle cancellations or refunds. Hosted verification and normal purchasing are not activated in the current development configuration; local or Apple Sandbox testing is not a claim that public purchases are available.

Apple handles its own account and payment information under its App Store privacy notice.

4. Advertising and consent

Load Parade’s current ad integration uses Google AdMob and Google’s User Messaging Platform (UMP) for test-only advertising and consent preparation. Requests are restricted to isolated, confirmed-adult development testing. Normal gameplay does not automatically initialize this test-ad provider.

Test ads can still involve data processing. When the advertising service is used, Google’s SDK may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, app/device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic or performance information. Processing depends on the SDK configuration and applicable choices. A test-ad label does not mean that no data leaves the device.

Current diagnostic requests disable publisher personalization and publisher first-party identifiers. Live personalized advertising is not enabled. We do not request precise GPS location for this ad implementation.

Where consent is required, the consent interface offers choices before eligible ad initialization or requests. You can refuse consent or manage options. Apple’s tracking permission, where applicable, is separate from UMP consent. Refusing optional tracking does not prevent you from playing local levels. Consent-message setup alone does not grant tracking permission.

Before live ads are introduced, the game’s privacy controls, audience safeguards, provider configuration, and disclosures must be updated to match the actual implementation. Ad availability is not guaranteed.

Further information: Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, and Google’s iOS advertising SDK data disclosure.

5. Support information

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, player identifier, app version, device/operating-system information, or screenshots. The in-game support action prepares an email for you to review and send; it does not silently submit a support ticket.

We use this information to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords, recovery secrets, payment-card details, or private authentication keys.

6. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by Apple, Google/Firebase/AdMob, and providers needed to deliver an enabled service or respond to support. We may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent fraud, or secure the service.

We do not sell your local game profile for money. Advertising-provider processing can fall within broader legal definitions of data sharing, depending on the feature, configuration, and jurisdiction; we do not treat this statement as an exemption from applicable privacy choices.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their applicable agreements and privacy notices describe their international-transfer safeguards. See Apple’s privacy information and Firebase’s privacy information.

7. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay records normally remain while you retain the profile on your device. Removing the app may remove local saves, but it does not automatically erase provider records, Apple purchase history, protected credentials, or any separately held support information.

We retain support and enabled online-service records only as needed for their stated purposes and applicable legal, security, or transaction obligations. A minimum transaction or abuse-prevention record may need to remain after other profile information is deleted, for example to prevent duplicate purchase delivery or process a refund. Providers may have separate retention periods.

You can request information or deletion using the contact below. We may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request relates to your profile, but will not ask you to email your password or recovery secret. A request is not a confirmation that deletion has completed. In-app cloud-account deletion is not yet available in the current local development version.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the services you use, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

Where applicable, information needed to provide a requested feature is processed to provide that service; security and fraud-prevention processing relies on legitimate interests; optional consent-based processing relies on your consent; and legally required records rely on applicable obligations. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

When available, use the game’s privacy-choice controls to revisit ad consent and iOS Settings to manage app tracking permissions. If a privacy control is not available in your development build, contact us; live advertising remains disabled in the current configuration.

9. Children and younger players

Load Parade is a colorful puzzle game. Its visual style is not an age-rating declaration. Current network-ad testing is limited to confirmed-adult development sessions; it is not authorization to track younger players.

We do not ask players to provide their real name or email address for local gameplay. Younger players should not send personal information through support without a parent or guardian’s involvement. A parent or guardian who believes a child has supplied personal information can contact us to request review and deletion where applicable.

Before online or live advertising features are released to younger audiences, the applicable audience settings, age-appropriate protections, consent requirements, and policy disclosures must be established.

10. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the features being provided, including access controls and protected authentication mechanisms for enabled online services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device protected and do not share private recovery or authentication information.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy when game features, service providers, or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest version. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before materially different processing begins.
