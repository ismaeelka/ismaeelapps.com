Privacy Policy

Last updated: 14 October 2026

Your game. Your privacy.

Load Parade is developed and operated by Ismaeel (“we”, “us”). This policy explains how we handle information when you play Load Parade, use an enabled online feature, or contact us.

Local first release: gameplay and progress use device-local saves. Google ads use child-restricted, non-personalized settings for all players after the required SDK privacy checks; TestFlight uses Google test ads. Apple processes purchases. This release does not use Firebase accounts, cloud-save synchronization, hosted purchase verification, remote configuration, push notifications, or game analytics/crash-upload services.

1. Game and profile information

Load Parade stores information needed to run the game, including a randomly generated player/profile identifier, level progress, Coins, Lives, worker upgrades, Ability inventory, time-based benefits, settings, and reward or transaction records. Local records help restore your progress and prevent the same action or reward from being applied twice.

Normal gameplay in this release keeps these records on your device. A player identifier is a support reference; it is not your real name and is not a password. The game does not require your real name or email address to play its local levels.

2. Online services and security

Puzzle play and saved progress work locally without a game account or hosted game service. Apple purchase/restore requests and permitted Google advertising/consent requests require a connection. Their providers receive the technical information needed for those requests.

Firebase Authentication, App Check, Analytics and Crashlytics, CloudKit recovery, public leaderboards, remote configuration and push services are not included in this local release. Owner/developer diagnostic tests in separate builds are not automatic collection from ordinary players.

3. In-app purchases

Apple processes payment through the App Store. We do not receive your payment-card number. The app verifies purchases using StoreKit on the device and keeps local product/transaction identifiers, purchase status, purchase time and delivery records to prevent duplicate grants.

Eligible permanent products can be restored through Apple. Local progress, consumable balances and used rewards do not have a hosted backup or cross-device recovery in this release. Apple Sandbox/TestFlight purchases are tests, not public charges. Refund/revocation reconciliation uses evidence available from Apple on the device; no game purchase-verification server is used.

Apple handles its own account and payment information under its App Store privacy notice.

4. Advertising and consent

Load Parade uses Google AdMob and the User Messaging Platform (UMP). The first App Store release applies child-restricted treatment, non-personalized advertising, and Google’s general-audience (G) ad content rating to every player. Publisher first-party identifiers are disabled. The game does not ask for or collect an age or birth date. Older TestFlight age preferences do not control this release’s ad eligibility.

Google consent information is refreshed each app launch using its restricted treatment for users under the age of consent. Where the SDK requires a privacy form, it must complete before ad initialization or requests. The Privacy page offers Google ad privacy options where required. The SDK determines whether an ad request is permitted; local gameplay remains available if advertising cannot run.

When consent/ad services are used, Google may process IP addresses and approximate location derived from them, app/device identifiers, ad views and interactions, and diagnostic/performance information. Test ads also involve provider processing; a test label does not mean that no data leaves the device.

The app disables publisher personalization and publisher first-party identifiers, does not request precise GPS location, and does not show an Apple tracking-permission prompt in this release. These settings do not eliminate Google's necessary service processing. See Google's disclosures below.

Video rewards are optional. The game stores a local reward receipt to deliver an earned benefit once; skipped or unearned videos grant nothing. Limited interstitial ads may appear between gameplay sessions, and a banner may appear on Home. Remove Ads suppresses interstitials and the Home banner, while optional rewarded videos remain a player choice. Ad availability is not guaranteed. TestFlight uses Google's sample ads; App Store builds use the publisher's units.

Further information: Google Privacy Policy, Google advertising information, and Google's iOS advertising SDK data disclosure.

5. Support information

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to send, such as your email address, message, player identifier, app version, device/operating-system information, or screenshots. The in-game support action prepares an email for you to review and send; it does not silently submit a support ticket.

We use this information to respond and investigate your request. Please do not send passwords, recovery secrets, payment-card details, or private authentication keys.

6. Sharing and international processing

Information may be processed by Apple, Google/AdMob, and providers needed to deliver an enabled service or respond to support. We may disclose information where required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, prevent fraud, or secure the service.

We do not sell your local game profile for money. Advertising-provider processing can fall within broader legal definitions of data sharing, depending on the feature, configuration, and jurisdiction; we do not treat this statement as an exemption from applicable privacy choices.

Service providers may process information in countries other than your own. Their applicable agreements and privacy notices describe their international-transfer safeguards. See Apple’s privacy information and Google’s privacy information.

7. Retention and deletion

Local gameplay records normally remain while you retain the profile on your device. Removing the app may remove local saves, but it does not automatically erase provider records, Apple purchase history, protected credentials, or any separately held support information.

We retain support and enabled online-service records only as needed for their stated purposes and applicable legal, security, or transaction obligations. A minimum transaction or abuse-prevention record may need to remain after other profile information is deleted, for example to prevent duplicate purchase delivery or process a refund. Providers may have separate retention periods.

You can request information or deletion using the contact below. We may ask for proportionate information to verify that the request relates to your profile, but will not ask you to email your password or recovery secret. A request is not a confirmation that deletion has completed. There is no hosted game account in this release. Local profile deletion is available in the game; it does not delete Apple purchase history, Google provider records or separately held support messages.

8. Your choices and rights

Depending on your location and the services you use, you may have rights to access, correct, delete, or obtain a copy of personal information, restrict or object to processing, withdraw consent, or complain to a data-protection authority. Contact us to make a request.

Where applicable, information needed to provide a requested feature is processed to provide that service; security and fraud-prevention processing relies on legitimate interests; optional consent-based processing relies on your consent; and legally required records rely on applicable obligations. Withdrawing consent does not undo processing that was lawful before withdrawal.

Use the game’s Privacy page to reopen Google privacy options where required. You can contact us about privacy requests. This release does not request Apple tracking permission.

9. Children and younger players

Load Parade is a colorful puzzle game. All players receive the same child-restricted, non-personalized advertising treatment; the game does not infer that a player is an adult. This treatment is not an App Store age-rating declaration. No birth date, real name or email is required for local gameplay. Ad providers may still process the technical data described above to deliver permitted contextual ads.

Younger players should not send personal information through support without a parent or guardian's involvement. A parent or guardian who believes a child supplied personal information can contact us to request review and deletion where applicable.

10. Security

We use safeguards appropriate to the features being provided, including access controls and protected authentication mechanisms for enabled online services. No storage or transmission method is completely secure. Keep your device protected and do not share private recovery or authentication information.

11. Policy changes

We may update this policy when game features, service providers, or data practices change. The date at the top identifies the latest version. Where required, we will provide additional notice or request consent before materially different processing begins.
