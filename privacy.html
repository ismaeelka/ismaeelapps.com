Privacy Policy

Can You Spot It?

Effective date: August 25, 2026

Last updated: August 25, 2026

Ismaeel Apps (“we,” “us,” or “our”) operates the Can You Spot It? mobile game for iPhone and iPad. This Privacy Policy explains what information may be processed when you use the game, why it is used, and the choices available to you. By using Can You Spot It?, you acknowledge the practices described in this Privacy Policy.

1. Information processed by the game

Gameplay and progression data

Can You Spot It? stores information needed to operate the game, including:

  • Completed, unlocked, and skipped levels
  • Stars and best completion times
  • Token balance and token transaction records
  • Tutorial completion status
  • Daily Challenge completion and reward status
  • Game settings such as sound and haptics
  • Purchase-delivery identifiers used to prevent duplicate rewards

Some information is stored locally on your device. If iCloud is available, eligible progression and economy information may also be synchronized through Apple CloudKit so your progress can be recovered or merged across your Apple devices.

We do not require you to create a separate Ismaeel Apps account.

Game Center

The game uses Apple Game Center for leaderboards, rankings, player identification, and score synchronization.

When you use Game Center, the game may access or submit:

  • Your Game Center display name
  • Your Game Center player identifier
  • Your leaderboard scores
  • Total stars
  • Best completion times
  • Daily Challenge completion times
  • Ranking and percentile information

Your Game Center nickname, avatar, scores, and other activity may be visible to other Game Center users according to your Apple Game Center privacy settings.

Apple manages Game Center accounts and related information. Learn more through Apple’s Game Center Privacy Notice .

In-app purchases and offer codes

Token packs and Apple Offer Codes are processed through Apple StoreKit and the App Store.

Apple processes payment credentials and billing information. We do not receive or store your complete payment-card details.

The game receives limited transaction information needed to verify and deliver a purchase, such as:

  • Purchased product identifier
  • Verified Apple transaction identifier
  • Transaction status
  • Token quantity associated with the purchased product

Verified transaction identifiers may be retained to prevent the same purchase from being credited more than once.

Purchases that are cancelled, unsuccessful, pending, or unverified do not provide tokens.

Rewarded advertising

The game uses Google AdMob to provide optional rewarded advertisements. Watching a successfully completed rewarded advertisement grants the reward described inside the game.

Google’s advertising services may process information such as:

  • IP address
  • Approximate location inferred from IP address
  • Device or advertising identifiers, when permitted
  • Advertisements shown and interactions with advertisements
  • App and product interactions
  • Device and operating-system information
  • Crash logs
  • Performance and diagnostic information

Advertising data may be used for delivering ads, measuring advertising performance, preventing fraud, analytics, and improving Google’s advertising services.

Where required, the game uses Google’s consent interface before requesting advertisements. The advertisements available to you may depend on your region, privacy choices, device settings, and Google’s policies.

The game does not award tokens merely because an advertisement screen opens or closes. Tokens are awarded only after the advertising provider sends a successful reward callback.

For additional details, see:

  • Google Privacy Policy
  • How Google uses information from sites or apps

Support communications

If you contact us by email, we may receive:

  • Your email address
  • Your name, if included
  • The contents of your message
  • Screenshots, device details, or diagnostic information you voluntarily provide

We use this information only to respond to your request, investigate problems, protect the game, and improve our services.

Please do not send sensitive personal information through support email.

2. Information we do not intentionally request

Can You Spot It? does not intentionally request direct access to:

  • Precise GPS location
  • Contacts
  • Photos or photo library
  • Camera
  • Microphone
  • Health information
  • Biometric information

Third-party services may process technical information as described in their own privacy notices.

3. How information is used

Information may be used to:

  • Save and restore game progression
  • Synchronize progress across devices
  • Maintain token balances and prevent duplicate rewards
  • Operate Daily Challenges
  • Verify and deliver purchases
  • Present rewarded advertisements
  • Submit Game Center leaderboard scores
  • Display player rankings
  • Diagnose errors and improve reliability
  • Respond to support requests
  • Prevent fraud, abuse, and unauthorized reward delivery
  • Comply with legal obligations

We do not sell personal information directly to data brokers.

Advertising providers may process information for advertising and measurement according to your consent choices, device settings, and their privacy policies.

4. Data storage and synchronization

Local game data is stored on your device using Apple-provided storage technologies.

Cloud-synchronized game data is stored through Apple CloudKit in connection with your iCloud account. CloudKit availability depends on your device, iCloud account, internet connection, and Apple services.

Game Center information and App Store transactions are managed by Apple.

Advertising information is processed by Google and its service providers according to Google’s policies.

5. Data retention

Local game information may remain on your device until you delete the app, erase its data, or use an available reset feature.

CloudKit information may remain associated with your iCloud account according to Apple’s storage and retention practices.

Transaction-delivery identifiers may be retained as long as reasonably necessary to prevent duplicate purchase or reward credits.

Support emails may be retained for as long as reasonably necessary to answer requests, maintain support records, prevent abuse, and meet legal obligations.

Third-party providers determine their own retention periods under their respective privacy policies.

6. Your choices

Depending on your device, region, and applicable law, you may be able to:

  • Change Game Center privacy settings through Apple Settings
  • Sign out of Game Center
  • Change advertising and consent preferences when offered
  • Limit personalized advertising through Apple device settings
  • Disable iCloud access for the game
  • Delete local game data by removing the app
  • Contact us regarding a privacy or deletion request

Disabling iCloud or Game Center may prevent synchronization, leaderboards, rankings, or other connected features from working correctly.

7. Children’s privacy

Can You Spot It? is a general-audience puzzle game.

We do not knowingly request children to provide personal information directly to Ismaeel Apps. Apple, Google, and other platform providers may apply age-appropriate protections based on account settings, region, and applicable law.

If you are a parent or guardian and believe a child has provided personal information directly to us, contact us at ismaeelapps@outlook.com so we can review the request.

The game currently uses services supplied by:

  • Google User Messaging Platform

These providers process information under their own terms and privacy policies. Their services may operate in countries other than the country where you live.

Apple, Google, and their service providers may process information in multiple countries.

Where required, these companies use legal safeguards for international data transfers according to their respective privacy policies and applicable laws.

10. Security

We use Apple’s platform security, verified StoreKit transactions, CloudKit private-database storage, and duplicate-delivery protections to help protect game information.

However, no electronic storage or transmission method can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

11. Your privacy rights

Depending on where you live, you may have rights concerning personal information, including rights to:

  • Request access
  • Request correction
  • Request deletion
  • Restrict or object to certain processing
  • Withdraw consent where processing depends on consent
  • Submit a complaint to an appropriate data-protection authority

Some information is controlled directly by Apple or Google. Requests involving those services may need to be submitted to the relevant provider.

To make a request concerning information controlled by Ismaeel Apps, email ismaeelapps@outlook.com .
